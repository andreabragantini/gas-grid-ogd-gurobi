Valves:
Pressure – Regulated pressure in (mbar)

The valves are similar to pipes, except that the output pressure will be fixed to the given value and the flow through the valve is not limited by any equation, but should be positive.

Alternatively, you could model them as a pipe with a very small K, i.e., there is no pressure loss.
